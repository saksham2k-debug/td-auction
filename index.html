About This Ring

A 3-carat Portuguese cut, framed by six marquise petals

The Portuguese cut is the world's brightest diamond shape — 161 facets that catch and return light from every angle. Mystical Petal pairs a 3 ct Portuguese centre with six marquise accents, set like petals around a tulip.

Design Story

Why "Mystical Petal"

161 facets mean every angle reflects light — bright even in candlelight. The marquise halo softens the centre without stealing from it. A ring designed to be looked at, twice.

Diamond Details

The centre stone

3.00 ct lab grown, IGI-certified, EF colour, VS clarity, laser-inscribed on the girdle — verifiable on igi.org. Six marquise accents add 0.63 ct (EF, VVS-VS).

Crafting

Made in Mumbai

14K or 18K gold — yellow, rose or white. Net weight 4.2–5.1g by size and karat. Hand-set 4-prong tulip setting. Lead time 7–10 days.

Wearing Notes

Built for every day

Low-profile petals that won't snag. Pair with a 1–2mm plain band for stacking, or wear alone. Resizable up to 2 sizes under Lifetime Exchange.
